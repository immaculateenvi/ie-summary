🏛️ CVE 105: Philosophy of Science & Technology

Definitions | Scientific Method | History | Environment

🔬 Definitions

Science: systematic knowledge via observation & experimentation
Technology: application of scientific knowledge for practical purposes

📜 Historical Development

Stone Age (flaked tools) → Bronze Age → Iron Age → Industrial Revolution (steam engine, Bessemer steel) → Information Age (computers, internet)

🧪 Scientific Method

Observation → Hypothesis → Experiment → Analysis → Conclusion → Theory

🌍 Man & Environment

Technology impacts: pollution, climate change, resource depletion → need for sustainable development
