Root Locus | Bode Plots | Gain/Phase Margin | Stability

Root Locus

Shows how poles move as gain K changes, breakaway point where dK/ds=0

Bode Plots

Magnitude and phase vs frequency, gain crossover (|G|=1), phase crossover (phase=-180°)

Stability Margins

Gain margin: measured at phase crossover, positive = stable
Phase margin: measured at gain crossover, positive = stable

Gain margin > 0, Phase margin > 0 → stable

Second Order Systems

ζ < 1 (underdamped), ζ = 1 (critically damped), ζ > 1 (overdamped)
Peak time: tp = π/(ωn√(1-ζ²)), Settling time: Ts = 4/(ζωn)
