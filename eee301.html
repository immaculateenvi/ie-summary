🔌 EEE 301: Electronics I

Ebers-Moll | Fermi-Dirac | MOSFET | BJT Models

📐 Ebers-Moll Model (BJT)

Ic = Ics(e^(Vbe/VT)-1) - αR Ics(e^(Vbc/VT)-1)

🌡️ Thermal Voltage

VT = kT/q ≈ 26 mV at T=300K

📊 Fermi-Dirac Distribution

F(E) = 1/(1 + e^((E-EF)/kT)), at E=EF → F(E)=0.5

🔌 MOSFET Types

D-MOSFET (channel exists at VGS=0), E-MOSFET (channel requires VGS > VT)
