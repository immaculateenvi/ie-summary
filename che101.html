🧪 CHE 101: General Chemistry I

Atomic Structure | Equilibrium | Kinetics | Quantum Numbers | Stoichiometry

Atomic Structure

Z = #protons, A = #protons + #neutrons
Isotopes: same Z, different A
Avg atomic mass = Σ (mass × abundance)
Periodic trends: radius ↓ across, ↑ down; IE ↑ across, ↓ down

Chemical Equilibrium

Kc = [C]ᶜ[D]ᵈ / [A]ᵃ[B]ᵇ, Kp = Kc(RT)^(Δn)
Q vs K: Q<K → forward, Q=K → equilibrium, Q>K → reverse
Le Chatelier: increase reactant → shift right; increase P → fewer gas moles

Kinetics

Rate = k[A]ᵐ[B]ⁿ
Half-life: first-order: t½ = 0.693/k
Arrhenius: ln(k₂/k₁) = (Ea/R)(1/T₁ - 1/T₂)

Quantum Numbers

n (shell), l (subshell: s=0,p=1,d=2), mₗ (orbital), mₛ (spin ±½)
Order: 1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d 4p 5s 4d 5p 6s 4f 5d 6p

Stoichiometry

n = mass/M, 1 mol = 6.022×10²³, Molarity = mol/L
Limiting reagent: smallest moles/coefficient determines product
