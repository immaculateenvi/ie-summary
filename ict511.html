Probability | CDF | Poisson | Bayes | Variance

Probability Basics

Probability cannot be negative, P(A^c) = 1 - P(A)
Mutually exclusive: P(A∪B) = P(A)+P(B)

CDF Properties

Non-decreasing, lim F(x)=1, right-continuous

Key Rules

Multiplication: P(A∩B) = P(A|B)P(B)
Bayes: updates prior probabilities with evidence
Law of Total Probability: P(A) = ΣP(A|B_i)P(B_i)

Distributions

Poisson: rare events, mean = variance = λ
Normal: 68-95-99.7 rule

Variance

Var(X) = E[X²] - (E[X])²
