🧲 ICT 304: Electromagnetic Field Theory

Coulomb's Law | Gauss | Maxwell's Equations | Vector Calculus

Electrostatics

E = Q/(4πε₀r²), V = Q/(4πε₀r), E for infinite line charge ∝ 1/r, infinite sheet: constant

Maxwell's Equations

∇·E = ρ/ε₀ (Gauss), ∇×E = 0 (conservative), ∇·B = 0, ∇×H = J (Ampere)

Vector Calculus

∇·F = divergence (net flux), ∇×F = curl, ∇²V = Laplacian
Stokes theorem: ∮F·dl = ∫(∇×F)·dS

Coordinates

Cylindrical: dl = dr a_r + r dθ a_θ + dz a_z
