Modulation | Line Coding | Channel Capacity | BER

Modulation

BPSK: Phase Reversal Keying (PRK)
OOK: On-Off Keying
8-PSK: 8 symbols, 3 bits/symbol
16-QAM: 16 symbols, 4 bits/symbol

Line Coding

Unipolar RZ: 1=+V pulse returning to zero
Polar NRZ: 1=+V, 0=-V
Bipolar RZ: alternating polarity for ones
Manchester: 1=high→low, 0=low→high

Channel Capacity

Shannon: C = 1.44 S/η (infinite bandwidth)
Sampling theorem: fs > 2fmax

BER

Pe = ½ erfc(√(Eb/(2η))) for BFSK

Multiple Access

Pure ALOHA: 18.4%, Slotted ALOHA: 36.8%
DSSS: Direct Sequence Spread Spectrum
