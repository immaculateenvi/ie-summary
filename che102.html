🧪 CHE 102: General Chemistry II

Alcohols | Carbonyl Compounds | Redox | Grignard Reactions

🍷 Alcohols

Primary (OH on C1), Secondary (OH on C2), Tertiary (OH on C3)
Oxidation: 1°→ aldehyde → acid, 2°→ ketone, 3°→ no reaction

🔑 Carbonyl Compounds

Aldehydes (terminal C=O), Ketones (internal C=O)
More reactive: aldehydes > ketones (less steric hindrance)

⚡ Grignard Reaction

RMgX + C=O → alcohol after H₃O⁺
Formaldehyde → primary alcohol, other aldehydes → secondary, ketones → tertiary

🧪 Reducing Agents

NaBH₄ (mild), LAH (strong) → reduce C=O to OH
