Stochastic/Deterministic Models | Distributions | Probability

Model Types

Stochastic: includes randomness and probability
Deterministic: no randomness, fixed outcome

Distributions

Exponential: time between events (rate λ)
Geometric: trials until first success
Poisson: rare events, mean = variance = λ
Binomial: discrete data

Probability Rules

P(A∩B) = P(A|B)P(B) (multiplication)
Independent events: P(A∩B) = P(A)P(B)
Bernoulli trial: two outcomes (success/failure)

Population Models

dP/dt = (β-δ)P → P(t) = P₀e^(kt)
Logistic growth: includes carrying capacity K
