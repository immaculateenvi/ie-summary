🌾 CSP 201: General Agriculture

Animal Classification | Breeds | Terminology | Management Systems

🐄 Animal Classification

Ruminants: cattle, sheep, goats (4-chamber stomach)
Monogastrics: pigs, poultry (simple stomach)
Pseudo-ruminants: horses, rabbits (cecum digestion)

📝 Terminology

Ram (male sheep), Ewe (female sheep), Lamb (young), Farrowing (pig birth), Calving (cattle birth), Lambing (sheep birth)

🏠 Management Systems

Intensive (confined), Extensive (free range), Semi-intensive (combination)

🌍 Nigerian Breeds

White Fulani (cattle), Yankasa (sheep), West African Dwarf (goat)
