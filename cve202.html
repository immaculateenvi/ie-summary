📏 CVE 202: Strength of Materials

Stress-Strain | Beam Deflection | Torsion | Pressure Vessels

📐 Stress-Strain

σ = F/A, ε = ΔL/L, E = σ/ε (Young's modulus), ν = -ε_lat/ε_axial

📊 Beam Deflection

Cantilever end load: δ = PL³/(3EI), Slope = PL²/(2EI)
Simply supported UDL: δ_max = 5wL⁴/(384EI) at center

🌀 Torsion

τ = Tr/J, θ = TL/(GJ), J = πd⁴/32 (solid shaft)

🎈 Thin Cylinders

Hoop stress: σ_h = Pd/(2t), Longitudinal stress: σ_l = Pd/(4t)
