📡 ICT 401: Antenna Theory

Wave Propagation | Dipole Antennas | Gain | Transmission Lines

Wave Propagation

γ = α + jβ (attenuation + phase), skin depth δ = 1/α

Antennas

Half-wave dipole: λ/2 length, max radiation at θ=90°, impedance ≈ 73 Ω
Isotropic antenna: ideal, radiates equally in all directions

Antenna Parameters

Gain: ratio of radiated power to input power
Directivity: Umax/Uavg
Radiation resistance: Rr = Prad/I²
Beamwidth: angle between half-power points

Transmission Lines

Z₀ = √((R+jωL)/(G+jωC)), v = 1/√(LC), SWR = Vmax/Vmin
Reflection coefficient: Γ = (ZL - Z0)/(ZL + Z0)

Friis Equation

Pr/Pt = GrGt(λ/4πr)², EIRP = Pt × Gt
