⚡ PHY 101: General Physics I

Kinematics | Newton's Laws | Work-Energy | Rotational Dynamics

Kinematics

v = u + at, s = ut + ½at², v² = u² + 2as
Projectile: T = 2u sinθ/g, H = u² sin²θ/(2g), R = u² sin2θ/g

Newton's Laws

F = ma, W = mg, f_k = μ_k N, f_s ≤ μ_s N
Centripetal: F_c = mv²/r

Work, Energy, Power

W = F·s·cosθ, KE = ½mv², PE = mgh
Work-Energy: W = ΔKE
Power: P = W/t = Fv

Rotational Dynamics

ω = ω₀ + αt, θ = ω₀t + ½αt², τ = Iα, I = Σmr²
KE_rot = ½Iω², L = Iω (conserved if τ_ext=0)
