📊 MTS 316: Partial Differential Equations

Laplace | Heat | Wave | Classification | Fourier Series

📐 Classic PDEs

Laplace: u_xx + u_yy = 0 (elliptic)
Heat: u_t = α u_xx (parabolic)
Wave: u_tt = c² u_xx (hyperbolic)

📊 Classification

B² - 4AC < 0 → elliptic, = 0 → parabolic, > 0 → hyperbolic

🎵 Fourier Series

f(x) = a₀/2 + Σ[aₙ cos(nπx/L) + bₙ sin(nπx/L)]
Odd functions: sine series only, Even functions: cosine series only

📈 Separation of Variables

Assume u(x,t) = X(x)T(t), reduces PDE to ODEs
