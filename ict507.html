🔌 ICT 507: Electrical and ICT Installation

Electrical Standards | Cable Selection | Voltage Drop | Safety

Standards & Regulations

IEC (international), BS (British), IEE (Institution of Electrical Engineers), NERC (Nigeria)

Cables

Wire: single conductor, Cable: multiple conductors
Insulation = electrical protection, Mechanical protection = physical

Voltage Drop

Vd = mV/A/m × I × L / 1000
V_load = V_supply - Vd

Example: Vd = 6.4×24×18/1000 = 2.76V, V_load = 230 - 2.76 = 227.24V

Testing

Insulation test, Continuity test, Earth leakage test
