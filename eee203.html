📊 EEE 203: Measurement & Instrumentation

Bridge Circuits | Errors | PMMC | CRO | Accuracy

Bridge Circuits

Wheatstone bridge: null method, balanced when galvanometer current = 0
AC bridges: measure impedance (inductance, capacitance)

Null method assumes zero current and zero voltage at balance

Measurement Errors

Gross: human mistakes
Systematic: consistent, reproducible
Random: unpredictable variations

PMMC Instruments

Measure DC current and voltage, uses permanent magnet and moving coil

CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope)

Displays waveforms; subsystems: CRT, vertical amp, time base, horizontal amp, trigger, power supply

Measurement Concepts

Accuracy: closeness to true value
Precision: repeatability
Resolution: smallest detectable change
Sensitivity: output/input ratio
