🔦 EEE 508: Optical Communication

Optical Fibers | WDM | Connectors | Faraday Rotators

Optical Fiber

Total internal reflection guides light
Graded-index: core diameters 50 or 62.5 μm
Attenuation = scattering + absorption

Connectors & Splices

Connectors: SC, ST, FC, LC (0.2 dB loss if air gap)
Splices have less attenuation than connectors

WDM

Wavelength-division multiplexing, DWDM spacings: 200, 100, 50 GHz

Optical Components

Isolator: transmits light in one direction (Faraday rotator)
Circulator: routes light sequentially through ports
