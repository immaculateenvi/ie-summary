AC Power | Rectifiers | BJT | Amplifiers

⚡ AC Power

P = VI cosφ (active), Q = VI sinφ (reactive), S = VI (apparent), pf = cosφ

🔌 Rectifiers

Half-wave: efficiency 40.6%, PIV = Vm, ripple frequency = f
Full-wave: efficiency 81.2%, PIV = 2Vm (center-tap), ripple frequency = 2f

🔋 BJT

α = Ic/Ie (0.95-0.99), β = Ic/Ib (50-200), β = α/(1-α)

🎚️ Configurations

Common emitter: high gain, medium input impedance; Common base: low gain, low impedance; Common collector: unity gain, high impedance
