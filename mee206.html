Laws of Thermodynamics | Entropy | Carnot Cycle | Heat Pumps

📜 Laws of Thermodynamics

Zeroth: thermal equilibrium → temperature concept
First: ΔU = Q - W (energy conservation)
Second: entropy of universe increases, heat cannot flow from cold to hot without work

⚙️ Thermodynamic Processes

Isothermal (T=const), Isobaric (P=const), Isochoric (V=const), Adiabatic (Q=0)

🔁 Carnot Cycle

Efficiency η = 1 - TC/TH, most efficient reversible cycle

❄️ Heat Pump & Refrigerator

COP_R = QL/W, COP_HP = QH/W = COP_R + 1
