💧 PHY 103: Properties of Matter

Viscosity | Surface Tension | Elasticity | Gas Laws | Fluid Statics

Viscosity

τ = μ(du/dy), ν = μ/ρ
Stokes' law: F = 6πμrv, v_t = 2r²(ρ_s-ρ_f)g/(9μ)
Poiseuille: Q = πr⁴ΔP/(8μL)

Surface Tension

γ = F/L, Capillary rise: h = 2γ cosθ/(ρgr)
Excess pressure: drop: ΔP=2γ/r, bubble: ΔP=4γ/r

Elasticity

Y = (F/A)/(ΔL/L), G = (F/A)/(x/L), K = -P/(ΔV/V)
Hooke's law: stress ∝ strain
Poisson's ratio: σ = -lateral strain/longitudinal strain

Gas Laws

Boyle: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂, Charles: V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂, Combined: P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂
Ideal gas: PV = nRT, v_rms = √(3RT/M)

Fluid Statics

P = P₀ + ρgh, F_b = ρVg (Archimedes)
Pascal: F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂
