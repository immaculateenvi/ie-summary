🛰️ ICT 403: Satellite Communication

Geostationary Orbits | ALOHA | TDMA | CDMA | Uplink/Downlink

Satellite Orbits

GEO: 24h period, 36,000 km altitude, 42,000 km radius
MEO: lower latency than GEO

Links

Uplink: Earth → Satellite, Downlink: Satellite → Earth

Multiple Access

Pure ALOHA: 18.4% efficiency
Slotted ALOHA: 36.8% efficiency
TDMA: time division, reference burst for synchronization

CDMA

Better interference rejection, FH-CDMA (frequency hopping), TH-CDMA (time hopping)
