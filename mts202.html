📊 MTS 202: Numerical Analysis

Root Finding | Interpolation | Integration | ODE Solvers

🔍 Root Finding

Bisection: linear convergence, f(a)f(b)<0
Newton-Raphson: xₙ₊₁ = xₙ - f(xₙ)/f'(xₙ), quadratic convergence
Secant: no derivative needed

📈 Interpolation

Lagrange polynomials, Newton's divided difference

∫ Numerical Integration

Trapezoidal: ∫f(x)dx ≈ (b-a)[f(a)+f(b)]/2
Simpson's 1/3: ∫f(x)dx ≈ (b-a)/6 [f(a)+4f(m)+f(b)], requires even intervals

📉 ODE Solvers

Euler: yₙ₊₁ = yₙ + h f(xₙ,yₙ), RK4: 4th order accuracy
