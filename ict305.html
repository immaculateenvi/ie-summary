🔌 Kirchhoff's Laws

KCL: Σ I_in = Σ I_out (charge conservation)
KVL: Σ V = 0 around closed loop

📊 Nodal Analysis

Based on KCL, supernode for voltage sources between non-reference nodes

🌀 Mesh Analysis

Based on KVL, supermesh for current sources between meshes

💾 Capacitors & Inductors

Capacitor: C = Q/V, energy = ½CV², open circuit at DC
Inductor: V = L dI/dt, energy = ½LI², short circuit at DC
