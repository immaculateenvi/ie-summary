📚 GNS 103: Information & the Library

Classification | Information Retrieval | Citation | Digital Literacy

📖 Library Classification

Dewey (DDC): 000-999 (10 main classes)
Library of Congress (LC): A-Z letters (e.g., Q=Science, T=Technology)

🔍 Information Retrieval

Boolean operators: AND, OR, NOT
Truncation: * (educat* → educate, education)
Primary sources: original research; Secondary: analysis (textbooks)

📝 Research & Citation

APA: (Author, Year) - social sciences
MLA: (Author Page) - humanities
IEEE: [1] - engineering

💻 Digital Literacy & Academic Integrity

CRAAP test: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose
Plagiarism: using others' work without credit → always cite!
