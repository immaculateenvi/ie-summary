📖 GNS 102: Use of Library & Study Skills

Term Paper Sections | Library Skills | Research Methods | Study Skills

Term Paper Sections

Introduction: Background, definitions, aims, scope, thesis
Main Body: Literature review, methodology, results, tables/graphs
Conclusion: Summary of findings, implications
Recommendations: Suggestions for action
References: List of cited sources

• Introduction defines key terms and states purpose
• Main Body presents evidence and data
• Conclusion summarizes findings

Library Skills

OPAC (Online Public Access Catalog), Dewey Decimal Classification (DDC), Library of Congress (LC), reference materials, serials

Research Methods

Interviews, questionnaires, observation, case studies, literature review, data analysis, inductive/deductive methods

Study Skills

Time management, note-taking (Cornell method), SQ3R (Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review), exam preparation
